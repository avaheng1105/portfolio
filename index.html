Projects

Things I’ve built.

Two projects that took raw, messy data all the way to a dashboard people use to make decisions.

01 · Equity advisory

Shareholding analysis platform

From raw register to client report

Analysts spent hours classifying thousands of holders in raw shareholder registers each period. I built a web platform that does it automatically and turns each register into a client-ready dashboard.

  • Upload Excel or CSV registers; automated workflows clean and validate them
  • A fund dictionary maps each holder to substantial shareholder, management, local fund, foreign fund or retail
  • Dashboards show the ownership breakdown, the GLC, local and foreign split over time, and top buyers and sellers between periods
  • One-click PDF export for client reports

02 · Yinson Greentech ChargEV

EV charging behaviour segmentation

Who charges, where and when

To understand who uses the charging network and how, I clustered members by where, when and how they charge, and turned the results into personas the business could plan around.

  • Prepared operational and user data with SQL, keeping only members with more than two completed sessions
  • Applied K-Means and KNN to find six charging personas, from workplace AC chargers to interstate business travellers
  • Profiled each persona on distance, DC versus AC use, weekend and working-hour patterns
  • Built Power BI dashboards to track performance and user activity
  • SQL
  • K-Means
  • KNN
